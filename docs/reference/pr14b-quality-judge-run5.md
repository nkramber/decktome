## The judge's reasons

- 1. lifegain Commander, any card: graded bad, judged typical. This is the standard community build of Karlov lifegain-tribal: solid payoffs (Heliod, Archangel of Thune, Vito, Well of Lost Dreams, Ocelot Pride) with reasonable removal and three wraths, plus a premium free spell in Solitude. But the mana base is 24 basics with only a handful of duals and essentially no real ramp or fast mana, and much of the roster is filler lifegain bodies rather than tutors, protection, or fast wincons. It plays its game fine but lacks the speed and sharp edges of a tuned list.
- 2. aristocrats Commander, owned first: graded typical, judged good. Card quality is strong for a casual-to-focused build: premium cheap removal (Swords, Fatal Push, Get Lost, Infernal Grasp), efficient draw (Skullclamp, Night's Whisper, Painful Truths), Orcish Bowmasters, and a real amulet/-1/-1 counter payoff package (Blowfly Infestation, Contagion Clasp, Archfiend of Ifnir, Massacre Girl) that plays to Denethor's Amass/counter plan. The mana base is functional but land-light and basic-heavy for a two-color deck with no real fixing needs, and a few filler bodies (Arcade Cabinet, Phantom Train, Bill the Pony) dilute the curve. Solid, on-plan, and interactive enough to win a league, but not cEDH-tuned.
- 3. artifacts Commander, bracket 4: graded bad, judged typical. The artifact-matters shell is coherent " " with Urza's Saga, Mystic Forge, KCI, Metalworker, Emry and cost reducers, but it's diluted by draft-level filler (Panther Robot, Chrome Steed, Memory Guardian, Jhoira's Familiar, Water Whip) and clunky interaction like Reality Ripple and Into Thin Air. The mana base is safe but unambitious " 22 Islands with essentially no fast mana beyond Mox Opal, where tuned Urza lists run Crypt/Vault/Grim Monolith plus Force of Will and real tutors. Solid, on-theme, but no sharp edge " the average community build of the commander.
- 4. dinosaur tribal, bracket 2: graded bad, judged typical. This is the standard community build of Gishath dino tribal: solid ramp package, Sol Ring/Signet, dual lands and tribal lands for a fine three-color mana base, plus haste and protection for the commander. But the creature suite leans on weak filler (Nest Robber, Dromosaur, Drowsing Tyrannodon, Pterodon Knight, Soaring Sandwing) rather than the format's strongest payoffs, and interaction is thin with almost no targeted removal or counterplay. Sound and on-plan, but no sharp edge.
- 5. blink Commander, owned first: graded typical, judged typical. This is a solid but unfocused mono-white blink/protection build: good staple removal (Swords, Get Lost, Generous Gift, wraths) and reasonable ramp, but the payoffs pull in three directions at once — blink ETBs, equipment/Puresteel, and +1/+1 counter growth — without a tight engine or a way to close games quickly. The mana base is mostly basics padded with pain and fixing lands (City of Brass, Spire of Industry, Exotic Orchard, Marsh Flats) that do nothing in a mono-color deck, and there's little of white's actual top end like Smothering Tithe, Enlightened Tutor, or stax pieces. It plays like the average community build of the commander: reasonable, functional, no edge.

- 9. Standard midrange, FNM: graded bad, judged bad.
