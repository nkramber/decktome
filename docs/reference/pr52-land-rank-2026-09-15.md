# PR-52, the land rank and the basic land swap, 2026-09-15

The roadmap gate of PR-52 reads:

> Gate: a test fails on the old rank for a fetch land and a tapped dual land. A replay of the Gríma request puts a fetch land on the shortlist. Its mana pass swaps a basic land for a better land of the pool. A whole deck gate run reads PASS against run 19, and the quality gate reports the three numbers (guardrail 14).

This document holds every free number of the item. The paid runs of D-738 come last, and the section at the end holds them.

Every count here reads the local card snapshot of 2026-09-04 and the local quality model. The deployed app reads a newer snapshot and a newer model, so a deployed number can differ by a little.

## What the code does

- **The rank.** `capLands` ranked the lands of the mana half by the deck colors a land makes, and then by play (D-450). A Commander land now reads its class first, at every bracket (D-733, D-734). Inside a class the deck colors lead, and then an owned land. A land of another format keeps the old order.
- **The class.** `profile.LandClassOf` reads the mana abilities of a land, and not the produced mana alone.
- **The swap.** At Commander brackets 4 and 5 the mana pass trades a basic land for a better land of the pool. The land is an untapped dual, a fetch land, or a land that enters untapped on a condition.
- **The budget.** An unowned land that costs more than the whole budget ranks under every class (D-736). A request with no budget reads no price.
- **F-146.** The untapped land step of the mana pass read the first 8 untapped lands of the pool by the alphabet. It ranks them now.
- **F-147.** The profile counted a fetch land as a source of every deck color. It counts the colors that the land can find now (D-735).

## The class order (D-733)

| Class | Reads | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Untapped dual | Two or more deck colors from abilities that cost {T}, or {T} and life, with no condition and no drawback | Watery Grave, City of Brass, Mount Doom, Brightclimb Pathway |
| Fetch | A search with no mana cost that puts a land of two or more deck colors onto the battlefield untapped | Polluted Delta, Prismatic Vista |
| Untapped on a condition | A condition that a deck meets: a basic land type, a count of lands, or a count of opponents | Sunken Hollow, Drowned Catacomb, Fabled Passage, Starting Town |
| Mana on a condition | A cheap cost with a condition on the line, or a cost of {1} or one hybrid mana | Exotic Orchard, Cavern of Souls, Opal Palace, Fetid Heath |
| Tapped dual | Enters tapped, or on a condition that most decks miss | Thriving Isle, Path of Ancestry, Evolving Wilds, Secluded Glen |
| Other | One deck color or none, a color that costs more, or a drawback | Marsh Flats in blue-black, Ash Barrens, Cascading Cataracts, Glimmervoid |

A weak condition is a creature type to reveal, behold, or control, or a life total of 13 or less. A drawback is a land that a text returns, control that it loses, or an untap step that it skips.

## The defects that the free checks found inside PR-52

The first class read the produced mana of Scryfall, and the replays refuted it. Five fixes followed, and each one has a test.

1. **Junk lands read as duals.** Scryfall lists every color for Lotus Vale, Gemstone Mine, Glimmervoid, Survivors' Encampment, and Cascading Cataracts. The class reads the mana abilities now.
2. **Ash Barrens and Demolition Field read as fetch lands.** Ash Barrens puts its land into the hand, and the search of Demolition Field costs {2}.
3. **Typal conditions read as easy to meet.** The swap put Temple of the Dragon Queen and Secluded Glen into a deck of ninjas.
4. **The swap dropped the basic land of the wrong color.** The capped ratios tie when every color meets its need, and the name order then dropped an Island.
5. **The swap could starve a fetch land.** It left Prosper with 2 basic lands beside Prismatic Vista and Fabled Passage.

## Limits

- The class reads rules text. A card with rare text can take the wrong class.
- The prices are a daily estimate of one snapshot, and the index holds no price for a few cards.
- The replay of the Gríma request reads the local snapshot and the local model. The deployed app read other ones.
- The bracket gate lane runs the pass over stored decks. It proves the swap, and it proves no build.
