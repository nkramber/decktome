A reader with no collection never gets the pool question, and the build reads any-card for them (D-37). The renderer read the unset slot as none: 6 pool rule misses on the six conversations with no collection.
- 3 misses were wrong expectations. "blink with a thin library" and "a commander the library does not hold" name a budget in their fourth message, and that message never goes out: the net of D-351 closes the budget question after two turns of silence, and the slots are full after turn 3. - 1 miss is a wrong slot, F-36. "poison in a tournament" opens with "An infect deck for a Modern tournament", and the second message says "Modern. The best deck under budget." The classifier replaced the theme `infect` with `the best deck under budget`. The expectation stays, and the miss names the defect on the next run.
