# PR-58: a card whose text names the creature type of a row

Date: 2026-09-20. Card snapshot: `.local/gcs/mtg-local-cards/scryfall/20260904T210157`, the snapshot of 2026-09-04. Pool: the 31,830 Commander-legal cards of a playable layout. Decisions: D-769 and D-770. Finding: F-144.

This file holds every count that PR-58 rests on. Each count comes from a free scratch test over the local snapshot. No paid target ran (D-770).

## 1. The gap

D-768 left one half of F-144 open. A type row of `themes.json` reads four things: a payoff tag, a payoff phrase such as "zombies you control", the subtype, and the typal tags of D-759 and D-765. It reads no card that only names the type in its text. Army of the Damned makes thirteen Zombie tokens, and no signal of the zombies row read it.

The generic rule gives a singular type word a text needle of that word (D-731). So "zombie" read such a card and "zombies" did not.

CAUTION: the "non-" prefix is the smaller part of the noise. Mikaeus, the Unhallowed punishes the Human type, and his text also holds a bare "Human". So the "non-" rule does not reach him.

## 3. What the needle adds, per class

The needle gives a signal to 786 cards that no signal of a type row reaches now. A scratch test put each card in one class. The first class that matched wins, in this order.

| Class | Cards | Example |
|---|---|---|
| The word sits near "token" | 550 | Army of the Damned |
| The word sits near "becomes" or "in addition" | 87 | Nim Deathmantle |
| The word sits after "non-" alone | 55 | Repel the Abominable |
| The word sits in the card's own name alone | 15 | Black Cat, Cunning Thief |
| The rest | 79 | Undead Slayer |

So 637 of the 786 reward the type, and 149 do not. The 79 of the last class are mostly cards that punish the type, such as Undead Slayer, Tivadar's Crusade, and Blazing Torch.

CAUTION: each class is a text window of 60 bytes, not a rule. The class of a single card can be wrong. The counts show the shape of the pool, and the replay of section 4 measures the effect.

A shortlist holds about 295 cards, and the cap does not move. So the needle makes a swap and no growth. The replay built one Commander shortlist per row, at bracket 2, with the whole card pool.

## 5. The word boundary

A text needle of `themes.json` matches by substring. A short type word sits inside a common word.

| Word | Substring | Whole word | Extra | Example of the extra |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| cat | 165 | 55 | 110 | "indicate", "automaton" |
| angel | 165 | 85 | 80 | "changeling" |
| elf | 156 | 128 | 28 | "Angelfire" |
| dragon | 224 | 211 | 13 | "Dragonfire" |
| goblin | 215 | 213 | 2 | "Hobgoblin" |
| human | 307 | 306 | 1 | "Humanity" |
| spirit | 343 | 342 | 1 | one card |

The other eight rows read the same count both ways. The land needle of D-759 matches on a word boundary for this reason. So the type needle does too, and the generic rule drops its substring needle for a type word (D-769). That corrects the singular word, which shipped the substring needle until now.

## 6. The singular and the plural

D-731 records that "zombie" and "zombies" read two different lists. The needle closes the text half of that gap. A scratch test scored every Commander-legal card under each type row and under its singular word. The two read the same set of cards with a score above zero, for all 15 rows.

The scores still differ on 143 cards over the 15 rows, because a row holds payoff phrases and the generic rule holds the slugs `w-matters` and `synergy-w`. That difference does not move a shortlist. The shortlists of the plural and the singular match card for card: zombies 295 of 295, goblins 295 of 295, spirits 296 of 296, and cats 295 of 295.

## 7. The method

Each count comes from a scratch test of the package `go/internal/candidates`. The copies sit in `.local/f144/`, and they never enter git.

- `zz_scratch_f144_test.go` counts the classes of section 3.
- `zz_scratch_f144b_test.go` writes each shortlist to a file, for the replay of section 4.
- `zz_scratch_f144c_test.go` compares each type row with its singular word, for section 6.
- A Python script over `oracle_cards.jsonl.gz` made the tables of sections 2 and 5.

CAUTION: a signal buys no shortlist slot. Army of the Damned takes the needle and reaches no shortlist of the replay, because it costs seven mana. The snapshot test of PR-58 names five cards that the replay proves enter.
