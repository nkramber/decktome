# PR-14C sources: MTGTop8, MTGGoldfish, and Aetherhub (2026-09-03)

This note records every fact the PR-14C readers rest on (D-482, D-490, D-502 to D-505). A session verified each one on 2026-09-03 with the app's own client agent, `mtg-deck-builder/0.1`. Re-verify before you change a parser. The trimmed pages of that day sit in `go/internal/meta/testdata/`.

## MTGTop8

- `https://www.mtgtop8.com/format?f=MO` is the Modern format page, 82 KB. It holds five sections: the metagame breakdown, the last major events, the last 20 events, the most played cards, and related links. The codes are `MO`, `ST`, and `cEDH`. `EDH` is Duel Commander and stays out (D-112).
- An event row is `<tr class=hover_tr>` with an online or a paper mark (`/graph/online/mtgo.png` or `/graph/online/paper.png`), a link `href=event?e=90258&f=MO` with the event name, one to three `/graph/star.png` marks, and a date `29/08/26`. The attribute values carry no quotes.
- The last 20 events paginate through `?f=MO&meta=54&cp=2` and so on, four pages for Modern, three for Standard and cEDH on 2026-09-03. The `meta` number names the current metagame window.
- An event page, `event?e=90258&f=MO`, holds the event name in `<div class=event_title>`, the field as `570 players`, the date, and one row per deck: `<div class=chosen_tr>` or `<div class=hover_tr>`, a place in `<div class=S14>` such as `1` or `3-4`, a deck link `href=?e=90258&d=885065&f=MO` with the archetype name, and `<a class=player>` with the player. The Brisbane Spotlight held 64 rows.
- An MTGO event page names its source: `Source: <a href=https://www.mtgo.com/decklist/...>mtgo.com</a>`. The MTGO lane holds those lists, so the reader stores the page and reads no deck of it (D-504).
- `mtgo?d=885065` is the text export of one deck: `N Name` lines, a `Sideboard` line, and the sideboard. A cEDH export lists the commander under the sideboard, as MTGO does. The lines end in CRLF. The `dec?d=` export carries set codes and is not read.
- The tier rule (D-505): a top-8 finish in a field of 32 or more is great, and the rest is good. A page that names no field takes the stars of the listing, two or three for a field of that size.

## MTGGoldfish

- `https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/custom/modern` is the user-deck listing, "User Submitted Decks" in the site's menu. It is server-rendered, 30 decks a page, and it read `Displaying 1 - 30 of 14675 in total` on 2026-09-03, 490 pages. `?page=2` is the next page. `/deck/custom/standard` has the same shape.
- A tile holds `href="/deck/7938665#paper"`, the deck name, and `by <author>`. The listing names no date.
- A deck page, `/deck/7938665`, is 56 KB. `<p class='deck-container-information'>` holds `Format: Modern`, `User Submitted Deck`, and `Deck Date: Sep 3, 2026`. A hidden form field, `deck_input[deck]`, holds the whole list: `N Name` lines, a `sideboard` line, and the sideboard. A second field, `deck_input[name]`, holds the deck name. The card table itself loads by script, and `/deck/component` answers 422 to a plain client.
- `/deck/download/<id>` answers 200 with the text list, and the robots file disallows it for every agent. The reader never fetches it (D-503).
- `/decks/user?format=modern` answers 404. `/metagame/modern` answers 200 and holds the tournament archetypes, which the MTGO lane covers.

## Aetherhub

- `https://aetherhub.com/Metagame/Modern` and `/Decks/Modern` answer 403 to the app's agent, with the Cloudflare page "Just a moment...". The house rule stands: no spoofed browser (D-470). The owner dropped the lane (D-502).

## The lanes

- The MTGTop8 lane walks the format pages of the three codes and their later pages, reads every paper event the store lacks, then every deck export the store lacks, up to 300 requests a run. The raw store keeps `raw/mtgtop8/event_<id>.gz` and `raw/mtgtop8/deck_<id>.gz`. A page that does not parse goes under `raw/mtgtop8-failed/`, and the next run fetches it again (M-6).
- The MTGGoldfish lane walks the listing pages of Modern and Standard, newest first, and reads every deck page the store lacks, up to 200 requests a run and 100 listing pages a format. The walk goes past a page whose decks are all stored, so a later run reaches older decks. The raw store keeps `raw/mtggoldfish/deck_<id>.gz`.
- `worker -meta -meta-reparse` re-reads the stored pages of both lanes and fetches nothing.
