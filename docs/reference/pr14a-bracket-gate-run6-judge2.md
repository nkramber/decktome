Judge: bracket 4. ### 12. Bracket 4, Prosper, Tome-Bound, treasure

Judge: bracket 4. This is a highly optimized Prosper build: eight-plus Game Changers (Ad Nauseam, The One Ring, Imperial Seal, Vampiric/Demonic Tutor, LED, Mox Diamond, Mana Vault, Chrome Mox) plus Sol Ring, Lotus Petal, Mox Opal, Dark Ritual and Wheel of Fortune for explosive starts. ### 13. Bracket 5, Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy, combo

Judge: bracket 5. This is a cEDH Kinnan build: heavy fast mana (LED, Moxen, Grim Monolith, Lotus Petal, Sol Ring), a full fetch/dual/pain manabase, free and cheap counterspell suites (Fierce Guardianship, Flusterstorm, An Offer You Can't Refuse), and tutors like Mystical Tutor, Intuition and Muddle the Mixture. It packs numerous two-card infinite mana combos (Devoted Druid, Basalt Monolith, Kinnan + untappers like Freed-style Filigree Sages/Voltaic Construct/Staff of Domination) closing into Thassa's Oracle/Lab Man/Jace wincons. With roughly ten Game Changers and a pure competitive combo-control plan, it plays far past Bracket 4 norms.

### 14. Bracket 5, Yuriko, the Tiger's Shadow, ninjas

Judge: bracket 5. This is a tuned cEDH Yuriko list: over a dozen Game Changers, a full fast-mana suite (Lion's Eye Diamond, Mana Vault, four Moxen, Lotus Petal, Dark Ritual, Culling the Weak), the best tutors (Imperial Seal, Vampiric, Demonic, Mystical) and free interaction (Force of Will, Fierce Guardianship, Pact, Force of Negation, Deadly Rollick). It runs Ad Nauseam into Thassa's Oracle as a compact win and an efficient dual-heavy mana base, with Yuriko ninjutsu clocks killing from turn three or four. That is metagame-focused competitive play, not a theme deck.

### 15. Bracket 5, Najeela, the Blade-Blossom, warriors combat

Judge: bracket 5. Najeela is a classic cEDH commander with her own infinite combat/mana loop, and this list is loaded with fast mana (Lion's Eye Diamond, Mana Vault, Chrome Mox, Mox Diamond/Opal/Amber, Lotus Petal, Dark Ritual, Sol Ring) plus a dozen-plus Game Changers including a full tutor suite and the Underworld Breach/LED and Thassa's Oracle win packages. Free interaction like Fierce Guardianship, An Offer You Can't Refuse, and Concerted Defense is there to protect turn-two-or-three kills, not to grind casual games. This is an optimized, metagame-facing competitive build rather than a themed warrior-tribal deck.
