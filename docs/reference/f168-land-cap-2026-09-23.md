This record holds each measurement of PR-69 (F-168, D-843). Each replay is free. It reads the local card snapshot of 2026-09-04 and the local quality model `20260923T202806Z`, which #217 wrote.

## 1. The premise

`docs/reference/f166-commander-rate-2026-09-23.md` says that the land cap drops four core lands of Najeela at each boost: Underground Sea, Savannah, Exotic Orchard, and Boseiju, Who Endures. A core card is a card in half or more of the TopDeck lists of the commander. That sweep read 64 Najeela lists.

The model of bracket gate run 11 reads 69 Najeela lists. On that model the cap keeps Underground Sea and Savannah, and it drops two other lands. The cause is the same.

## 3. The owner choice

The session gave three fixes, and the owner chose the first (D-843):

- **A.** A bracket 5 request with a commander rate fills the mana half in score order. The half keeps its 20 places, and the total cut of D-835 keeps it first.
- **B.** A land at a rate of 0.5 or more skips the cap. The cap of this list grows to 44, and the pain lands stay.
- **C.** A bracket 5 land cap of 45. The mana half grows to 23 places, and the score order adds two lands alone. So Exotic Orchard and Boseiju still miss the cap.
