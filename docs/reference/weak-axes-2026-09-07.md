# The weak axes of the quality model (2026-09-07)

This note is the plan for the precon bar of the quality gate, under its bar since run 8 (D-486). It reads quality gate run 13 and the code of `go/internal/quality`, and it proposes one measurement item and three pull requests. The owner answered the three questions at the end on 2026-09-07 (D-568). Every number here comes from `docs/reference/pr14b-quality-gate-run13.md` on 2026-09-07.

## The evidence

The precon bar asks the model to score a real precon above a broken copy in 0.95 of the pairs. Run 13 reads it under the bar in every format.

| Format | Precon over bad | Weak axis | Pairs of the axis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commander | 0.78 | synergy 0.78 | 20,000 of 24,288 |
| Standard | 0.88 | colors 0.38 | 8 |
| Modern | 0.86 | colors 0.51, copies 0.89 | 3,233 and 3,599 |

The other axes read 0.92 or better. Four Commander weights point against the sense of their feature: `draw` -0.23, `wipe` -0.15, `land` -0.26, and `commander_decks` -0.13. The tier judge lane agrees with the model on 7 of 25 built decks (run 4, D-515). The explain mode grades 19 of 24 built decks bad (D-488). The largest contributions against every built Commander deck are `card_rate`, `source_spread`, `fast_mana`, and `tapped_share`.

## The mechanism

**The corpus reads the tournament lists alone.** `tierWeight` in `go/internal/quality/corpus.go` gives a great list a weight of 2, a good list 1, and every other list 0. So the card rates and the pair lifts come from the great and the good lists, and a typical or a baseline list adds nothing. In Commander those lists are the cEDH lists of Topdeck.gg and the database. A precon's cards mostly read the prior, and its pairs mostly lift nothing. Its synergy-broken copy reads much the same. So the two strongest features, `card_rate` at +0.98 and `synergy`, separate the two by little, and the detector reads the shape alone.

The same corpus explains the weights against sense. The ladder measures the distance to a cEDH list, so more lands, more draw, more wipes, and a popular casual commander read as lower quality. It explains the built decks too. A themed deck from a collection holds few cEDH cards, so it grades bad on the corpus features whatever its shape (D-488).

**The pair bar reads a subset.** `MaxPairChecks` caps a bar at 20,000 pairs, and the loop walks the holdout precons in order. Commander holds 44 holdout precons and 1,150 bad lists, 50,600 pairs, so the bar reads the first 17 precons alone. Modern holds 61 and 540, 32,940 pairs, so the bar reads the first 37. The per-axis bars read every precon, except the Commander synergy axis at 24,288 pairs, which stops at 36. The weighted mean of the Commander axis bars is about 0.87, and the overall bar reads 0.78. So the 17 precons it reads are the worse ones. A bar over a subset is a measurement defect, whichever way it errs.

**The Standard bar rests on one precon.** Standard holds 8 precons inside its three-year pool (D-478): 7 in train and 1 in the holdout. The bar reads 50 pairs of one deck, and its colors axis 8 pairs. One list in five by hash (D-473) leaves a rung of 8 with one holdout member, or none.

**The colors axis of the 60-card formats.** The break turns every land that fixes two colors or more into a basic of the first color of the identity, in W-U-B-R-G order. `color_sources` is the worst color's share of the sources it needs, so a starved color shows in it. The 0.51 of Modern is a cross-pair effect, or the detector misses the broken copies, and run 13 can not say which. The pairs cross every precon with every broken copy. A weak intro pack graded bad meets the broken copy of a Challenger deck that still holds its staples. The diagnostic of M-7 reads the misses and names the case.

**The copies axis of Modern.** `playset_share` and `singleton_share` both carry a negative weight, -0.56 and -0.66, and the copies break moves them in opposite directions. The two features measure one thing twice, and the break cancels in part.

## The plan

The order is measure, then fix, one concern per item (guardrail 10). Every item runs the free quality gate, and the two Commander items run the tier judge lane, $0.36 each (run 4). No bar moves (D-486), and no weight changes by hand.

### M-7: The axis diagnostic and the honest bars

The gate document gains one section per format. For each axis it lists the pairs the bar misses. Each miss shows the feature deltas between the precon and its copy, the detector probability of each, and the ladder score. It names the three features the break moves least, which is where the break is invisible. It says for each miss whether the detector passed the copy or flagged the precon.

The pair bars sample evenly. Every holdout precon meets the same number of bad lists, drawn by a seeded hash, under the same cap of 20,000. The per-axis bars sample the same way.

The small rungs read out of fold. The baseline and the typical lists split into five folds, the fit runs once per fold, and the bars read every list as holdout once. The stored model stays the fit of the main split. The gate takes about five minutes instead of one.

Gate: quality gate run 14, free. The bars can move in either direction, and the document names the mechanism per weak axis with numbers. The run 14 bars are the reference for PR-29 to PR-31. OQ-73 asks the owner for the go.

### PR-29: The casual corpus

The corpus keeps two groups. The top group is the great and the good lists, as today, and it gives `card_rate`, `unseen_share`, and `synergy`. The casual group is the typical and the baseline lists, and it gives `casual_rate` and `casual_synergy` the same way. In Commander the casual group is the 854 average decks and the 189 precons. In Modern it is the 175 MTGGoldfish decks and the 327 precons, and in Standard 68 and 8. The features read the training split alone (D-473), and the fit reads the new features as it reads the rest.

Then a precon and its synergy-broken copy separate on `casual_synergy`, because the precon's pairs sit in the casual lists and the copy's do not. The ladder gains a casual reference, so a deck reads its distance to the casual lists and to the tournament lists both. The four weights against sense have a reason to move.

Gate: the Commander synergy axis reads 0.95 or better, and no axis falls under its run 14 read. The tier judge lane over deck gate run 16 reads the built decks, $0.36. The count graded bad in the explain mode falls under 19 of 24. The judge bar of 80 percent stays the bar. `MetaBoost` and the bracket 5 offer read `card_rate` and the signal as today, so no deck gate runs (D-474).

### PR-30: The commander reference (waits on OQ-74)

EDHREC gives the average deck of each commander, and the store holds it as a typical list with the commander's slug. `commander_rate` is the share of a deck's nonland cards the average deck of its own commander holds. A deck with no average deck reads the feature's mean, as an absent value does. The reason sentence names it: "few of the cards people play with this commander".

Gate: the free quality gate with no axis under its PR-29 read, and the tier judge lane, $0.36. The count graded bad falls again, or the document says why not.

### PR-31: The colors and the copies axes of the 60-card formats

The colors break starves the color the spells need most. It reads the pips of the deck and turns the lands that fix into basics of the color with the smallest requirement. The copies pair becomes one feature: `distinct_share`, the distinct nonland cards over the nonland copies, replaces `playset_share` and `singleton_share`. The diagnostic of M-7 decides the rest. A detector that misses the copies gets a threshold per axis, and a flagged precon gets a read of why.

Gate: the Modern colors axis and the Standard colors axis read 0.95 or better over the five folds, and the copies axis 0.95 or better. No Commander bar falls.

## The read of run 14 (2026-09-07, M-7 built)

M-7 is done (D-571), and `docs/reference/pr14b-quality-gate-run14.md` is its gate, 98 seconds under five folds. Run 14 replaces run 13 as the reference (D-568). The build found F-55 on the way: every copy that fell to synergy took the key of the synergy copy, and read its profile.

| Format | Cross bar, run 13 | Cross bar, run 14 | Own copy, run 14 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commander | 0.78 of 20,000 | 0.86 of 99,933 | 0.88 of 945 |
| Standard | 0.88 of 50 | 0.87 of 730 | 0.97 of 40 |
| Modern | 0.86 of 20,000 | 0.85 of 99,754 | 0.99 of 1,635 |

**The cross bar mixes two questions (F-56).** Each precon beats its own broken copies almost always, and the cross bar still fails, because the precon rung spans bad to good. In Modern, 7,264 of the 9,226 colors misses are pairs the detector passed on both sides. A weak product graded bad loses to the broken copy of a Challenger deck. The ten precons that lose most in Modern are theme and intro decks of 2004 to 2014. In Commander they are the products of 2011 to 2015, all graded bad. Both the ladder and the detector read an old product as worse than a broken new one. The owner chose the own copies as the bar (D-573), and the cross pairs stand as information.

**Commander synergy is the one axis weak on its own copy**, 271 of 389. The break moves `card_rate` by 0.08 standard deviations, and `synergy` and `unseen_share` by 0.9 each, under weights of +0.17 and -0.23 (F-53, measured). Of the 118 own misses, 54 are the ladder's and 64 involve a precon the detector flags. So PR-29 stands, and the detector's read of the old precons joins it.

**The 60-card breaks are visible on their own copies.** Modern reads colors at 74 of 75 and copies at 61 of 61, and Standard colors at 3 of 4. The colors break moves `tapped_share` by 1.27, `color_sources` by 1.03, and `source_spread` by 0.96. So PR-31 fixes a break that already works on its own copy, and it parks (D-573).
