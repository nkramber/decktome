## Findings

### P2-1: A card with no eligible priced printing keeps an unrelated price

Status: fixed in `ba42d54`.

Open at: `b57de37f6f072e43b82a4ccc7e1dc6310ca293e5`.

File: `go/internal/cards/index.go:288-295`.

Trigger: the Oracle card has a positive `PriceUsd`, but its printings have no priced, playable paper printing. This includes a card whose priced printings are digital or marked `NotForPlay`.

Expected: D-1057 defines the card price as the cheapest paper printing that a player can play. Protobuf says zero means no price.

Actual: At `b57de37`, `cheapest` had no entry, so the index kept `c.PriceUsd` from the Oracle card or the earlier paper swap.

Consequence: the budget, model, and buy cost can treat an ineligible printing's price as the price of a playable copy.

Correction: `ba42d54` clears the price and its date when no eligible priced printing exists. Its regression case covers a positive Oracle price and only ineligible or unpriced printings.

Regression check: `go test ./internal/cards -run '^TestCardPriceIsCheapestPlayablePrinting$'` passes. The response file records that the test fails on `b57de37` with prices of 300 and 4.

### P2-2: Whole-deck budgets ignore unpriced owned cards

Status: open.

Open at: `fe25a215dd9c2f1a246839d2b059bc3fa89b71c6`.

File: `go/internal/generate/generate.go:562-563`.

Trigger: `BudgetWholeDeck` is true, a deck contains an unpriced card with an owned copy, and the priced total stays under the budget.

Expected: D-238 counts every copy for a whole-deck cap. D-1060 says a card with no price counts as over the budget.

Actual: `assemble` always calls `UnpricedToBuy`. That function counts only a line where `count > owned`, so an unpriced owned card does not set `n`. `DeckCostWith` also adds no price for it.

Consequence: the deck gets no `over_budget` finding or repair turn, although the whole-deck value is unknown and D-1060 requires the card to count over budget.

Correction: count every unpriced deck card for a whole-deck cap, and count only unpriced copies to buy for a buy-list cap.

Regression check: add a `BudgetWholeDeck` case with an unpriced owned card and a priced subtotal below the cap. Assert that the deck gets the `over_budget` warning.
